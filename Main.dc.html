NASA Technology Transfer · TOP2-246 · ARC-17171-1

Portable Medical Diagnosis Instrument

A hand-held, four-sensor diagnostic device (CNT breath array, breath condenser, miniaturized DMS, and CD-based centrifugal microfluidics) built to give astronauts comprehensive in-flight health assessment on deep-space missions.
